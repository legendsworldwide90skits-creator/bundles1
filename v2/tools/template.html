BEFORE YOU ASK

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A collector’s purchase starts with a conversation. Here are a few useful starting points.

Can I choose individual shirts?

Yes. Select the pieces you like and send us your list. A curated bundle is different: Legends proposes the pieces based on your brief.

Where is the stock held?

Across Legends’ sourcing network in the region shown at the top of the page. Pieces can sell fast, so a displayed piece is not a reservation. Legends confirms availability before finalising the proposal.

What is included in the price?

Prices are per piece and drop automatically as your bundle grows. Shipping is calculated separately. Your final value includes the total, shipping and any destination charges.

What if a piece doesn’t pass the quality check?

At the video quality check stage, any piece can be swapped for another one from the stock. If needed, Legends will agree the applicable refund with you.

Does every shirt have an authenticity certificate?

Not necessarily. Photos, videos, verification and certificates where applicable are different forms of support. Specific provenance or match use requires evidence for that individual piece.
